Bring a pot of well-salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.

While it cooks, warm the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes and cook gently until the garlic is pale gold and fragrant — do not let it brown, or it turns bitter.

Add the drained pasta to the skillet along with a splash of the reserved pasta water. Toss hard for a minute so the starchy water and oil emulsify into a light sauce that coats every strand.

Turn off the heat, add the parsley, and toss again. Loosen with a little more pasta water if needed.

Serve right away. Add a dusting of parmesan if you like, though the classic version stays dairy-free.
