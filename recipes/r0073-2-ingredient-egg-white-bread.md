Preheat the oven to 350°F / 175°C and line or grease a loaf pan.

Whip the egg-white powder and water in a stand mixer until stiff peaks form.

Add the thick Greek yogurt and mix for about 30 seconds, just until incorporated.

Transfer to the prepared loaf pan and bake for 30-40 minutes.

Cool before slicing. The creator suggests toasting slices and says individual slices can be frozen and thaw quickly.
