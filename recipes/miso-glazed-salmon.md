Whisk the miso, mirin, soy sauce, maple syrup, and ginger into a smooth, glossy glaze.

Pat the salmon dry and set it skin-side down on a parchment-lined sheet pan. Spoon about two-thirds of the glaze over the tops, reserving the rest.

Let the fillets marinate at room temperature for 10 minutes while the oven heats to 425°F (220°C).

Roast for 10–12 minutes, until the salmon flakes at the thickest part and the glaze has caramelized at the edges.

For extra color, switch to the broiler for the final 1–2 minutes and watch closely so the sugars don't scorch.

Brush the fillets with the reserved glaze, then scatter with scallions and sesame seeds. Serve over steamed rice with a quick cucumber salad.
