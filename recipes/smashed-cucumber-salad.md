Lay the cucumbers on a board and smash them firmly with the flat side of a knife or a rolling pin until they split. Smashing creates craggy surfaces that grab the dressing far better than clean slices.

Tear or cut the smashed cucumbers into rough bite-size pieces and toss with the salt. Let them sit in a colander for 10 minutes to draw out excess water, then pat dry.

Whisk the garlic, rice vinegar, tamari, sesame oil, and chili crisp together in a serving bowl.

Add the drained cucumbers and toss to coat. Let everything mingle for 5 minutes so the flavors soak in.

Finish with the toasted sesame seeds and an extra spoonful of chili crisp for anyone who wants more heat. Serve cold.
