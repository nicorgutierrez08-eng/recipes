If needed for a smoother crisp, blend or mash the cottage cheese.

Portion or spread it in thin, separated rounds on a lined tray and season with Vegeta.

Bake at 200 C / 392 F for 30-40 minutes, watching closely near the end.

Cool fully so the chips firm and crisp before lifting from the tray.
