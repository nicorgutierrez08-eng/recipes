Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it well. Cook the spaghetti until al dente, reserving at least a cup of the starchy pasta water before draining.

While the pasta cooks, add the diced guanciale to a cold, large skillet and set it over medium heat. Cook slowly until the fat renders and the pieces are golden and crisp, about 8 minutes. Turn off the heat.

In a bowl, whisk the whole eggs, egg yolks, grated pecorino, and black pepper into a thick paste.

Add the drained hot pasta to the skillet with the guanciale and toss to coat in the rendered fat. Let it cool for 30 seconds — too hot and the eggs will scramble.

Pour in the egg mixture along with a splash of pasta water, tossing vigorously and constantly. Keep adding pasta water a little at a time until the sauce turns glossy and clings to every strand.

Serve immediately with an extra shower of pecorino and black pepper.
