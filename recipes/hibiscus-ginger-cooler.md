Combine the hibiscus, ginger, cinnamon stick, sugar, and 3 cups of the water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer for 8 minutes until deeply ruby and fragrant.

Turn off the heat and let the mixture steep for another 10 minutes. The longer it sits, the more tart and floral it gets, so taste as you go.

Strain the concentrate through a fine sieve into a pitcher, pressing on the solids to extract every drop of color.

Stir in the remaining 3 cups of cold water and the lime juice. Taste and adjust — more sugar for softer, more lime for sharper.

Chill thoroughly, then serve over plenty of ice with a lime wheel. For a grown-up version, top each glass with a splash of sparkling water or a shot of mezcal.
