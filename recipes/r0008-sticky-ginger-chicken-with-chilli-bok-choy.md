Cook the rice and prepare the bok choy.

Sear the chicken thighs until browned and nearly cooked through.

Add ginger, garlic, chilli flakes, honey and dark soy. Cook until the sauce reduces to a sticky glaze and the chicken is cooked through.

Quickly cook or stir-fry the bok choy. Serve with rice and chicken, then finish with sesame seeds, spring onion and extra chilli.
