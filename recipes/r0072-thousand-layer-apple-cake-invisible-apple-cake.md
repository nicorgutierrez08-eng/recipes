Heat the oven to 170°C / 340°F and line a loaf tin with parchment.

Peel, core and slice the apples paper-thin, about 1-2 mm. Toss with the orange zest and juice.

Whisk eggs and caster sugar. Add melted butter, milk, vanilla, cinnamon and nutmeg, then whisk in the flour.

Coat the apple slices in the batter. Pack them tightly into the loaf tin and pour over any remaining batter.

Bake for about 1 hour, covering with foil if the top browns too quickly. Cool 15 minutes in the tin, then cool fully. Brush with warm apricot jam and dust with icing sugar if using.
