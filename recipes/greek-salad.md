Add the tomato wedges, cucumber, bell pepper, red onion, and olives to a wide serving bowl.

Drizzle with the olive oil and red wine vinegar and season with a little salt. Toss gently — you want everything glossy, not broken down.

Lay the whole slab of feta on top (don't crumble it — that's the traditional way) and sprinkle the oregano over the cheese and salad.

Let it sit for 10 minutes so the juices, oil, and vinegar mingle into a dressing at the bottom of the bowl.

Serve with plenty of crusty bread to mop up the juices. Break the feta apart at the table.
