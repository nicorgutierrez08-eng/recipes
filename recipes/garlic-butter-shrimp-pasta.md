Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil and cook the linguine until just shy of al dente. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining.

While the pasta cooks, pat the shrimp dry and season with salt. Melt 2 tablespoons of the butter in a wide skillet over medium-high heat.

Sear the shrimp for about 90 seconds per side until just pink, then transfer them to a plate — they'll finish cooking later, so don't overdo it.

Lower the heat to medium. Add the remaining butter, the sliced garlic, and red pepper flakes, and cook gently until the garlic is golden and fragrant, about 2 minutes.

Pour in the white wine and let it bubble and reduce by half, scraping up any browned bits. Add the lemon zest and juice.

Add the drained pasta and a splash of reserved pasta water, tossing hard until the sauce turns glossy and clings. Return the shrimp to the pan, fold in the parsley, and serve at once.
