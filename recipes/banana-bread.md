Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 9x5-inch loaf pan with parchment.

Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat and keep cooking, swirling, until it turns golden and smells nutty, about 4 minutes. Pour it into a large bowl and let it cool for a few minutes.

Whisk the brown sugar into the browned butter, then the eggs and vanilla, until smooth.

Stir in the mashed bananas.

Add the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt, and fold just until no streaks of flour remain — don't overmix, or the loaf turns tough.

Scrape into the pan and bake for 55–65 minutes, until a skewer inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool in the pan for 15 minutes before turning out.
