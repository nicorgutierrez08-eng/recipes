Combine garlic, honey, avocado oil, soy sauce, vinegar and ginger. Coat the salmon and marinate for about 5-10 minutes; overnight is also possible.

Bake or air-fry the salmon for about 12 minutes, or until cooked through and lightly caramelized.

Toss cucumber with vinegar, sugar, sesame seeds and chili crisp to make the quick salad.

Build each bowl with rice, salmon and cucumber salad. Finish with creamy sriracha.
