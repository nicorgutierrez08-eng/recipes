Combine cilantro, lime, garlic, honey, salt, pepper, paprika and oregano.

Coat the chicken thighs thoroughly and arrange in a baking dish.

Bake at 415 F / 213 C for about 35 minutes, until cooked through and browned.

Rest briefly, then portion for serving or meal prep.
