Blend cottage cheese, Parmesan, tomato sauce, chipotle peppers, garlic and milk into a smooth sauce.

Season and cook the steak, then rest and slice or dice it.

Cook the protein pasta until just tender and reserve some pasta water if needed.

Combine pasta, sauce, butter and steak over gentle heat. Portion into 10 servings and finish with chives.
