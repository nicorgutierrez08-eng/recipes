Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss the squash, onion, and garlic cloves with the olive oil and a good pinch of salt on a sheet pan.

Roast for 30–35 minutes, until the squash is deeply tender and caramelized at the edges, turning once halfway through.

Squeeze the roasted garlic out of its skins and add it, the squash, and the onion to a blender (or a pot for an immersion blender).

Add the vegetable broth, nutmeg, and coconut milk. Blend until completely smooth, working in batches if needed.

Warm the soup through in a pot over medium heat, thinning with a little more broth if it's too thick. Taste and adjust the salt.

Serve with a swirl of coconut milk, a crack of black pepper, and toasted pumpkin seeds if you have them.
