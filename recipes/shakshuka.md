Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook until soft, about 8 minutes.

Stir in the garlic, paprika, and cumin and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

Pour in the crushed tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.

Use a spoon to make six wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each one. Cover the pan and cook for 6–8 minutes, until the whites are set but the yolks are still soft.

Scatter the feta and parsley over the top. Serve straight from the pan with crusty bread for scooping.

> Leave the eggs runny — the yolk becomes part of the sauce as you dip.
