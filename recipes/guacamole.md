Halve the avocados, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.

Add the lime juice and salt, then mash with a fork to your preferred texture — a little chunky is better than a smooth purée.

Fold in the onion, jalapeño, cilantro, and diced tomato.

Taste and adjust: more salt to make the flavors pop, more lime for brightness, more jalapeño for heat.

Press a piece of plastic wrap directly onto the surface if not serving right away, to keep it from browning. Serve with tortilla chips.
