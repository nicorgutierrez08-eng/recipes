Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, and a pinch of salt together in a large bowl.

In a second bowl, stir the ricotta, milk, egg yolks, lemon zest, and lemon juice until smooth.

In a clean bowl, whip the egg whites to soft peaks. This is the step that makes them cloud-light, so don't skip it.

Fold the wet ricotta mixture into the dry ingredients until just combined, then gently fold in the egg whites in two additions, keeping as much air as possible.

Melt a little butter in a skillet over medium-low heat. Ladle in the batter and cook until bubbles form and the edges set, about 3 minutes, then flip and cook 2 minutes more.

Keep the finished pancakes warm in a low oven while you cook the rest. Serve with a dusting of powdered sugar and a drizzle of honey or maple syrup.
