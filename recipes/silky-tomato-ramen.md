Warm the sesame oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the garlic and ginger and cook until fragrant but not browned, about 1 minute.

Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes until it darkens and smells sweet, then add the canned tomatoes, crushing them with a spoon.

Pour in the vegetable broth, bring to a simmer, and cook gently for 15 minutes. Blend the broth until silky using an immersion or countertop blender, then return it to low heat.

Whisk the miso with a ladle of hot broth until smooth, then stir it back into the pot. Add the tofu and warm through. Taste and adjust with a little salt.

Cook the ramen noodles in a separate pot of boiling water according to the package, then drain and divide between two bowls.

Ladle the tomato broth and tofu over the noodles. Top with the soft-boiled eggs if using and serve immediately.
