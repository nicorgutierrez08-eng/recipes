Divide the ground chicken into two portions and flatten each portion.

Wrap each portion completely around a mozzarella stick, sealing the edges.

Combine Parmesan with salt, pepper and garlic powder. Roll each chicken tender in the coating.

Bake or air-fry until browned and the chicken reaches a safe internal temperature. Let stand briefly before eating because the cheese will be very hot.
