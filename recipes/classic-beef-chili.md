Heat the oil in a large heavy pot over medium-high. Add the beef and cook, breaking it up, until well browned. Drain off most of the fat.

Add the onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic, chili powder, and cumin and cook for 1 minute.

Stir in the tomato paste and let it cook for another minute to deepen the flavor.

Add the crushed tomatoes, beans, and beef broth. Season with salt and bring to a simmer.

Lower the heat and simmer, uncovered, for 45 minutes to an hour, stirring now and then, until thick and rich. Add a splash more broth if it gets too dry.

Serve with your favorite toppings — sharp cheddar, sour cream, diced onion, or a handful of crushed tortilla chips. It's even better the next day.
