Spoon the thick cream from the top of the coconut milk into a hot pan or wok. Cook it with the green curry paste for 2–3 minutes until fragrant and the oil starts to separate.

Add the sliced chicken and stir to coat in the paste, cooking for a few minutes until it turns opaque.

Pour in the rest of the coconut milk, the fish sauce, and the sugar. Bring to a gentle simmer.

Add the bell pepper and green beans and simmer for 8–10 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are crisp-tender.

Turn off the heat and stir in the lime juice and thai basil. Taste and balance — more fish sauce for salt, more lime for brightness, more sugar to round it out.

Serve over jasmine rice.
