Warm the olive oil in a heavy pot or the sauté setting of an instant pot. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook until soft, about 6 minutes.

Stir in the garlic, cumin, and minced chipotles with their adobo sauce. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant and deeply aromatic.

Add the black beans and vegetable broth. Bring to a simmer and cook for 25 minutes so the flavors meld and the beans start to break down.

For a thicker, silkier soup, blend about a third of it and stir it back in — this gives body without any cream.

Finish with the lime juice and a big handful of cilantro. Taste for salt. Serve with avocado, tortilla chips, or a scoop of rice.

This freezes beautifully; cool completely and portion into containers for up to three months.
