Toss the chicken with the yogurt, 1 tablespoon of the garam masala, half the garlic and ginger, and a big pinch of salt. Let it marinate for at least 20 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).

Sear the marinated chicken in a hot, dry skillet in batches until charred at the edges but not fully cooked through. Set aside — it finishes in the sauce.

Melt the butter in the same pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft and golden, about 7 minutes, then stir in the remaining garlic, ginger, garam masala, and the cumin. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

Pour in the tomato passata and simmer for 10 minutes until it darkens and thickens.

Stir in the cream, then return the chicken and any juices to the pan. Simmer gently for 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce is silky. Taste for salt.

Finish with chopped cilantro and serve with basmati rice or warm naan.
