Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 9-inch cake pan with parchment. Brush the sides with a little olive oil.

Whisk the flour, cocoa, sugar, baking soda, and a pinch of fine salt together in a large bowl until no cocoa lumps remain.

Make a well in the center and pour in the olive oil, hot coffee, vinegar, and vanilla. The coffee deepens the chocolate flavor without tasting like coffee.

Whisk from the center outward just until the batter is smooth and glossy. It will be quite thin — that's exactly what keeps this cake so moist.

Pour into the prepared pan and bake for 38–42 minutes, until the top springs back and a skewer comes out with a few moist crumbs.

Cool in the pan for 15 minutes, then turn out onto a rack. Finish with a shower of flaky salt and, if you like, a dusting of cocoa. It keeps, well wrapped, for three days.
