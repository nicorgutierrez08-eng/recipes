Pat the chicken dry and toss it with the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, and a generous pinch of salt until every piece is coated.

Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high until it shimmers. Add the chicken in a single layer and let it sit undisturbed for 3–4 minutes so it browns before you stir.

Cook, tossing occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through and the edges are crisp and caramelized, about 8 minutes total. Squeeze the lime juice over the top and toss.

Warm the tortillas directly over a burner flame or in a dry pan until pliable and lightly charred.

Pile the chicken into the tortillas and finish with diced onion and a shower of cilantro. Serve with extra lime wedges.
