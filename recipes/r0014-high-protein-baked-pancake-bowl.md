Preheat the oven to 180°C (350-356°F).

Add the egg, yogurt, milk, flour, protein powder, sweetener and baking powder directly to an oven-safe bowl.

Mix until smooth and add the desired fruit or toppings.

Bake for 20-25 minutes, until risen and cooked through. Cool briefly and add an optional drizzle before serving.
