Separate the eggs, keeping the whites free of yolk.

Whip the egg whites with salt until stiff peaks form.

Combine the yolks with the Greek yogurt, then gently fold into the whipped whites.

Portion onto a lined tray and bake until set and lightly golden; the original temperature and time were not publicly visible.
