Preheat the oven to 190 C / 374 F, or 180 C / 356 F fan.

Whisk the egg and melted coconut oil. Stir in apple, cinnamon, oats, rice flour and baking powder.

Scoop heaped spoonfuls onto a lined baking tray.

Bake for 14-17 minutes, until set and slightly golden. Add optional toppings after baking.
