Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Brown the meat and combine with marinara.

Layer zucchini, meat sauce, cottage cheese and mozzarella in a baking dish. Repeat for a second layer.

Bake for about 30 minutes, until bubbling and the zucchini is tender.

Rest briefly before slicing so the layers hold together.
