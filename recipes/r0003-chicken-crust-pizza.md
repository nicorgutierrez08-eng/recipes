Mix the ground chicken, oat flour and garlic salt until evenly combined.

Press into a thin personal-pizza crust on a lined baking tray.

Bake until the chicken crust is fully cooked and firm enough to hold toppings.

Spread with pizza sauce, add mozzarella and return to the oven until melted. Finish with parsley.
